Pui Ching Ordering System

FAQ

Q1: Can I login into PCOS without using puiching email?

A: No, the PCOS can only be accessed via puiching gmail. We are sorry for the inconvenience.

Q2: What should I do when I encounter a bug?

A: You may report it via our Report Bug form, so our developers can deal with it. Thank you.

Q3: Can I propose any ideas?

Suggestions are always welcome. Feel free to give your advice here.


Q4: What will happen if I don't pay for my lunch?

A: You should pay for your lunch to avoid the risks of receiving penalties. Your lunch will be canceled but you will still need to pay for your lunch afterward.

Q5: Is there any handling fee?

A: No, there isn't any handling fee.

Q6: Which payment methods can be used?

A: You can pay for your meal during lunchtime. You are kindly reminded that you can only pay with Octopus if you order your lunch via PCOS.

Q7: Can I order lunches for my team?

A: PCOS now supports multiple purchases at a time. You can purchase at most 4 lunches in a single order.

Q8: Who has access to my order history?

A: Only developers, canteen staff and yourself can access your ordering history.
